# Licences and terms of the data

The code is under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`). The tables and network series in `data/` and `results/` were derived by the authors from third-party sources, and those sources' terms come first.

| What | Derived from | Licence or terms |
|---|---|---|
| Tables, statistics and fitted values the authors made (`results/`, `data/networks.csv`, `data/network_summary.csv`, `data/outcomes_7point.csv`, `data/splits/`, `data/feature_dictionary.csv`, `data/screening_decisions.csv`) | as below | CC BY 4.0, subject to the source terms below |
| UTD19 network series, detector lists and per-detector summaries | [UTD19](https://utd19.ethz.ch) | UTD19 is for academic, non-commercial use, and asks that its DOI be cited and that it be credited in the acknowledgements. It states no terms on redistribution. Only derived series and detector-level summaries are shared here; the raw detector records are not. |
| WebTRIS network series | [National Highways WebTRIS](https://webtris.nationalhighways.co.uk) | Listed as free and open; related data are under the UK Open Government Licence. |
| PEMS04 and PEMS08 network series | [ASTGCN repository](https://github.com/wanhuaiyu/ASTGCN), from Caltrans [PeMS](https://pems.dot.ca.gov) | Attribute both. |
| Portland network series | [PORTAL](https://new.portal.its.pdx.edu) | PORTAL states no terms. |
| Seattle network series | [Seattle Loop Data](https://github.com/zhiyongc/Seattle-Loop-Data), from the Washington State Department of Transportation | Attribute both. |
| Road attributes, `data/map_metrics.csv`, map averages in `data/features_per_network/`, and the map deposit (`data/RAW_SOURCES.md`) | [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org) | Open Database Licence (ODbL), © OpenStreetMap contributors. |
| Population by age and sex in `data/features_per_network/` | [WorldPop](https://www.worldpop.org) | CC BY 4.0. |
| Other covariates in `data/features_per_network/` | [GeoQuery](https://geo.aiddata.org) and the layers named in `data/RAW_SOURCES.md` | Cite GeoQuery and each layer. |

Where a source states no terms on sharing, the authors shared derived network-level data on the assumption that this is allowed. A data provider who disagrees can ask for its data to be removed through the journal during review, and through the contact in the paper afterwards.
